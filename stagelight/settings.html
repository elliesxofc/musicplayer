Stagelight

control OBS from your phone or any browser

1 · OBS on this PC

in OBS: Tools → WebSocket Server Settings → tick Enable WebSocket Server → Show Connect Info. copy the port and the password here. the password stays on this PC.

…

2 · accounts

everyone who opens the panel logs in with their own username and password. pick new passwords (not your OBS or email one), at least 10 characters.

    full access: everything, and the remote display of this whole PC (when it's switched on below). stream control: going live, recording, scenes, sources, audio and the layout. watch only: sees everything, changes nothing.

    3 · open the panel

    on a phone, tablet or PC on the same Wi-Fi / network as this PC, open:

    no home network found. is this PC connected?

    the first time, Windows may ask whether Stagelight can use the network: allow it on private networks.

    from anywhere (another house, mobile data): Tailscale
    1. install Tailscale (free) on this PC and sign in.
    2. install it on the phone or PC that controls the stream, signed in to the same account, or share this PC with your friend's account (Tailscale admin console → the PC → Share).
    3. an address starting with 100. shows up here. open it on that device, from anywhere.

    Tailscale keeps the panel off the open internet: only your devices (and people you share with) can reach it. don't open a port on your router for Stagelight.

    your own web address (optional)

    open the panel at your own address, like https://stage.lilianax.lol, from anywhere, without Tailscale. it goes through a free Cloudflare Tunnel on this PC (the guide has the steps). nothing is opened on your router.

    through the web address: logins only work over HTTPS, each visitor is locked out on their own after 5 wrong tries, and 30 wrong tries from the internet in 10 minutes pause web logins for 15 minutes (the home network and Tailscale keep working). the remote display also tries a direct connection over the internet, which works on many networks but not all.

    4 · remote display (the whole PC)

    like AnyDesk or Parsec, in the panel: see this PC's screen, and (if you allow it) use its mouse and keyboard, for when you need OBS's full window. only full access accounts can open it, and the panel asks for that account's password again before it starts. while someone is connected, a bar shows at the top of this screen with a stop button.

    with mouse and keyboard on, every full access account can use this whole PC, not just OBS. only give full access to people you trust with your PC, and switch this off when you don't need it.

    with file transfer on, every full access account can read and add files anywhere on this PC (after typing their password again). nothing can be deleted or overwritten from the panel. switch it off when you don't need it.

    mouse and keyboard work on Windows only; here the remote display is for watching.

    more

    pictures, videos and sounds uploaded from the panel are kept in Stagelight's own folder (OBS reads them from there: delete one only after removing it from OBS).

    closing this window keeps Stagelight running by the clock, so the panel keeps working. quit it from the tray icon.
